    Overview

    Classic Collection

    Four 21-storey towers — Blk 1, 3, 9 and 11 — bringing together comfort, ease and enduring design.

    Luxury Collection

    Two taller 30-storey towers — Blk 5 and 7 — closest to the Singapore Island Country Club, with elevated views across greenery and enhanced privacy. 4- and 5-bedroom homes come with a private lift.

    Facilities

    More than 80 curated lifestyle facilities across three distinctive clubs, set in a nature-integrated 5-hectare landscape.

    Wellness Club

    At the north-west end by Sin Ming Avenue, with tennis courts, a wellness pool and garden spaces.

    Grand Clubhouse

    The heart of the development, wrapped around a winding lagoon pool between the Luxury towers.

    Island Club

    Among the Classic towers by Bright Hill Drive, with its own pools, pavilions and play areas.

    Location

    Bounded by Upper Thomson Road, Bright Hill Drive and Sin Ming Avenue, with Upper Thomson MRT station (Thomson–East Coast Line) beside the Bright Hill Drive entrance.
